# Main Heading & Points

Residential real estate is undergoing a major transformation as sustainability becomes a priority for both developers and homeowners. Eco-friendly practices are no longer optional—they are shaping the future of housing by reducing environmental impact, lowering costs, and creating healthier living spaces.


![Blog Details](/images/blog/blogdetail-1.jpg)

Key practices include:
- Energy-Efficient Design 🔋
- Renewable Energy Sources ☀️
- Sustainable Building Materials 🪵
- Water Conservation Systems 💧
- Green Financing Options 💵
- Smart Home Integration 📲
- Community-Oriented Design 🌍
